Grading mechanistic explanations by weights-blind simulation

Libor "Burny" Burian, Victor Levoso. Developed in a mechanistic interpretability hackathon on automatically evaluating mechanistic interpretability explanations, co-organized by the authors in their community and its Discord server, mechinterp.com.

An LLM agent that has never seen the network and has no internet access reads an explanation of a one-layer modular-addition transformer (Nanda et al. 2023) and writes every weight of a replacement. The replacement is scored against the original by output KL (behaviour) and hidden-state similarity (HS) (cross-validated linear regression between the two networks' internal activations). Does the score track explanation quality? Code and preregistration · paper (PDF) · replacement networks and transcripts
