No finding overturns a measured result or the direction of an effect, and no stored HS, KL or p-value changes. The
audit found four problems:
- verdicts were labelled against a post-hoc rule rather than the preregistration;
- several claims had a narrower scope than their wording;
- one undisclosed flaw in the self-test ablation;
- a set of factual, stale and citation slips.

All are fixed in this branch.

## Findings and fixes

### Hypothesis evaluation

**1. The preregistered verdict was shown second (moderate).**

The rules:
- Prereg §5 allows "cannot tell at this N" only "when the test is undefined at the current N".
- §5 falsifies H2 if a comparison is "not significant after Holm".
- §6 says that at N = 3 a 3-vs-3 Mann–Whitney is defined but cannot go below 1/20, and grants no exception.

So by the preregistration, these are **"not supported"**:
- the four H2 comparisons (Holm p 0.20, 0.70, 0.20, 0.70);
- every per-target H2 replication (p = 0.05 against p < 0.05);
- T-new's H1 replication (JT p = 0.05).

What the documents did: the tables led with deviation 1's post-hoc "cannot tell at this N", a rule applied at N = 3
after the N = 3 verdicts were read. The literal reading appeared only in a parenthesis on the H2 row and not at all
in the replication rows. The docstring of `stats.verdict` called the rule "preregistered".

**Fix.**
- `stats.verdict_prereg`, `hypotheses.py` (`verdicts_prereg`), `predictions._verdict`, the replication rows and
  `site.py` now show the §5 verdict first and the post-hoc reading after it.
- The docstring is corrected.
- The prose is updated in the paper (§3.5, §4.7, §6), summary and README.

The effect sizes are unchanged: E3 beats W-freq and W-role with complete separation; W-alg and W-scramble tie.

**2. H7 is shown only against networks with other key frequencies (moderate).**

Evidence (`results/audit8/verify.json` → `shared_with_T0`):
- Of the trained wrong targets, T1, dev and T-paper share no key frequency with T0, and T2 shares one (37). T2 is
  every replacement's best wrong target.
- The other wrong targets (wd0, rand) have no clean mechanism.
- Among the retrains, the only one sharing a frequency with T0 (wd1_s8) has the highest HS, 0.329. The others score
  0.000–0.178.

So H7 does not test whether HS separates networks that use the same frequencies. W-alg and W-scramble suggest it does
not.

**Fix.** Scope added to the paper (abstract, §4.3, §6), the README highlight and the summary. A same-frequency
control is listed as I2 in `reports/next-steps.md`.

**3. H4b reading (minor).**

H4b's criterion is "any non-monotonicity". The HS half holds (1.000 → 0.888, strictly falling); the KL half fails.
The documents gave only the post-hoc reading ("did not test the grader as intended").

**Fix.** Everywhere, the preregistered reading (falsified; HS half holds, KL half fails) now comes first. The
post-hoc cause follows: accuracy dips to 0.977 after 100 steps and recovers, and KL tracks that change in function.

**4. New: the self-test ablation left a self-test cue (moderate).**

- `src/vmi/simulator/sandbox.py` removes `selftest.py` from the no-self-test variant, and strips its mention from
  `TASK.md` and the prompt.
- But the starter `sandbox_template/build_weights.py` is copied in every variant. Its line 3 says "re-run this script
  and selftest.py after each stage".
- All three reported no-self-test agents read that line (`logs/transcript.md` lines 226, 226 and 213 in their run
  folders, published on the HF repo). None tried to run `selftest.py`. All three wrote their own label checks.
- The paper said the ablation removed "the instruction to raise accuracy", and the code comment said "every mention".

**Fix.**
- Disclosed in §4.5 and Limitations; the comment is corrected.
- The starter stays unchanged, because the protocol is frozen across rounds.
- A clean variant is listed as I1 in `next-steps.md`.

### Claims beyond the evidence

**5. Memorisation cue.** The documents said the recall was "cued by … an outline of its training setup". Only E0's
text outlines the training setup. The E1 runs (5 of the 6 cases) saw the exact architecture (`ARCHITECTURE.md`) plus
the paper's own algorithm.

**Fix.** Generator text (`tables.py`, `predictions.py`), abstract, Limitations, summary, README.

**6. README premise and highlights.**
- "Computes the same intermediate quantities, the explanation captured the mechanism" is now hedged to the level the
  metric sees.
- The highlight "what makes the rebuild faithful" now reads "what raises HS", because the E1–E3 rebuilds are
  Pizza-like against a Clock target.
- "Specific to the network explained" gains H7's scope.

**7. HS against a weights-only baseline.** The paper's own embedding-spectrum cosine orders the T0 builds almost as
HS does. HS adds discrimination mainly for W-role. On this task, then, HS behaves largely as a frequency-overlap score.

**Fix.** Added to Limitations and to the Conclusion.

**8. "The agent exceeds the compiler in 4 of 11 pairs."**
- Two of the four margins are below 0.01: T1 E2 +0.007 and T-new E4 +0.001.
- If the compiler's value sat at the median of the agent's runs, all three runs would lie above it with
  probability 1/8, so about 1.4 of the 11 pairs would by chance.

**Fix.** Now stated next to the count (generated value `h5-small`). The "not a ceiling" claim rests on T2 E3 (+0.179)
and T2 E4 (+0.041).

**9. Fourier-restricted M (HS 0.992).** This shows that T0's non-key Fourier components carry little of the variance
HS weights. It does not show that "the key frequencies carry nearly all of what HS measures".

**Fix.** Reworded.

## Open risks (not fixable by editing)

- N = 3 per condition, one simulator (Claude Haiku 5.5), one task family. At this N, H2 cannot be supported by
  design: complete separation gives Holm p 0.20. Round 3 (N = 10) is postponed.
- HS behaves largely as a key-frequency-overlap score on this task. Below that level it did not separate the Pizza or
  scrambled-table builds. A same-frequency specificity control (next-steps I2) and a neuron-level or causal metric
  are needed.
- The "true" explanations are true at the frequency and role level only. Their rebuilds are Pizza-like.
- Agents repair explanations and check task accuracy in every run, so scores mix the explanation with the repair.
