### 2.3 Metrics and statistics code
KL direction KL(P_M ‖ P_M̂) (Conmy et al. 2023's convention); cross-validated ridge R² with training-fold means, pooled
over folds and dimensions, clipped to [0, 1], harmonic mean per site; linear CKA (Kornblith et al. 2019); Procrustes
2 − 2‖XᵀY‖_* after centring and Frobenius normalisation (Ding et al. 2021, eq. 7); signed matched-neuron correlation on
the target's live neurons (amendment 1 item 3; Li et al. 2016 match one-to-one); temperature calibration by golden
section (KL is convex in the scale); JT, Holm, Kerby's rank-biserial r, Wilcoxon, Kendall's W — all as in their sources.

### 2.4 Method implementations
Zhong et al.'s distance irrelevance (Def. 4.2) and gradient symmetricity (Def. 4.1) match the definitions; the
restricted loss matches Nanda et al. §5.1 (it reproduces ARENA's 5.5474e-8 on the paper model); the trajectory is
graded with the same grader (HS at step 40,000 equals the reported HS to five decimals). W-freq (seeded, disjoint from
the true set on all six targets), W-role (MLP and unembedding remapped to a disjoint set), W-scramble (frequency drawn
from the key set, phases uniform) and W-alg (Pizza at the target's frequencies) are built as §3 states. The reference
compiler reads only the explanation's spec (`pipeline.py` passes it nothing else and `compile.py` opens no file); the
spec adds only numeric precision to the text (three significant figures in the text, four in the CSV).

### 2.7 Bootstrap coverage
With three runs per arm the nominal 95 % percentile interval of a mean is the runs' range; it misses the median when all
three fall on one side (probability 1/4), so coverage is 75 %. A normal-data simulation gives 74.8 % for a mean and
80.3 % for a difference of two means (`results/audit5/checks.json`). The stated figures hold; they are our calculation,
not Hesterberg's (F5).

## 5. What a reviewer should still weigh

- **N = 3.** The verdicts that pass are H1 (HS), H3, H4, H7 and H8; H7's p is the smallest six replacements allow,
  and none of H4/H7 would survive a Bonferroni correction over eight hypotheses (reported in the paper).
- **What HS measures here** is frequency-level structure: the Fourier-restricted target scores 0.99, every network
  on the target's frequencies scores about 0.9 and every network on other frequencies scores below random networks.
  The preregistered secondary embedding-spectrum overlap, a frequency-only score, orders the nine T0 conditions
  similarly (Kendall τ 0.65) but rates W-role 0.53 where HS gives 0.20, so HS adds a component-level check that the
  embedding alone does not (`results/audit5/checks.json`, `frequency_only_baseline`). HS's value as a general grader is
  not shown by this task alone.
- **The true explanations are partial**: Pizza-like when built (Clock/Pizza), and on T0 inexact about frequency 2 (F1).
  The ladder therefore tests frequency-level content, which is what the paper now claims.
- **The agent repairs.** Every run checked task accuracy; the agent-free compiler is the only feedback-free reference.
