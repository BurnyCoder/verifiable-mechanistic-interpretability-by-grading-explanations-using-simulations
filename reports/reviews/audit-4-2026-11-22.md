## 2. Verdict summary

**No substantial methodological error invalidates the reported findings, and no verdict changes.** Every stored score
that comes from a weight file (261) and every stored test statistic and verdict (44 items, plus the replication table)
reproduces with code that shares nothing with the project's grader and statistics modules. The hypothesis verdicts
follow preregistration §5–6 and the disclosed deviations.

Two interpretations were wrong and are corrected (deviation 11):

1. **H4b** (planted mechanism) fails its rule because the planted model's *function* does not drift monotonically
   under further training; output KL follows the function. The abstract had read this as KL failing as a distance.
2. **H8** (trajectory) is weaker than worded. A post-hoc positive control shows that during circuit formation final T0
   itself, its Fourier-restricted version and the agent-free compiler build rise against T0's checkpoints exactly as
   the agent replacements do, and that the size of the preregistered whole-trajectory ρ is set by small drifts after
   grokking — final T0 itself gets the lowest ρ of all networks with T0's frequencies. H8 shows shared frequency
   structure, not a separate ability of HS to track circuit formation.

Also corrected: three-run bootstrap intervals cover about 75 %, not 95 %; several dozen citation, fact and code-comment statements
narrowed to what their sources say (none invented); twelve method and statistics references the paper relied on were
added; stale code comments; one count error; Clock/Pizza wording; two VRAM unit errors. Confirmed: no leakage through
the compiler or the E0/E1 texts; the contamination argument holds and is stronger than stated (the simulator was
released before the targets' files existed); no ties invalidate the exact tests; the transcript-based counts hold.

## 3. Findings on results, methods and hypothesis evaluation

### 3.1 Every stored score and every verdict reproduces

The independent grader reproduces all 261 stored scores that come from a weight file — 116 agent runs (development runs included), 55 reference-compiler builds, 72 H7 wrong-target gradings, 9 retrains, 3 distilled students, 5 random initialisations and the weights dump — with a largest difference of 1.3e-10 in HS and 1.9e-11 in KL, and the four planted-mechanism gradings to 1.6e-12 (`results/audit4/regrade.json`). The forward pass alone matches grokviz's logits to 6e-14 on T0. Two details the stored numbers hide: (i) 25 of the 696 directional R² values of the agent runs are negative before clipping (minimum -0.058), all in replacements without T0's frequencies (E0, E1, W-freq) or in development runs, so clipping only floors already-low scores; (ii) taking the total sum of squares about the held-out fold's own mean instead of the training-fold mean (§4 does not say which) changes no HS by more than 0.0009.

All 44 stored test statistics and verdict strings in `results/hypotheses_round2.json` reproduce exactly with the
independent tests (`results/audit4/hypotheses.json`, `all_match: true`): H1's exact JT p (HS) 9.47e-5 over 369,600
assignments and its Spearman ρ 0.907; H1's −KL trend p 0.931; the four H2 Mann–Whitney p-values (0.05, 0.35, 0.05,
0.5) and their Holm values; H4's p 1/220 and Holm 2/220; H4's two-sided student comparisons (p 0.1 = the smallest
attainable); H7's Wilcoxon p 1/64; H8's minimum ρ; every H5 agent − compiler difference. The replication table (T0
E2–E4, T1, T2, T-new, T-paper) also reproduces. The verdict rules are applied as written: "supported" for H1 (HS
part) needs JT p < 0.05 and a Spearman CI above 0 (both hold); H2 reads "cannot tell" because a 3-vs-3 one-sided
exact Mann–Whitney cannot go below p = 0.05 (deviations 1 and 7, following §6); H4 and H7 can reach significance
at their sizes (smallest p 1/220 and 1/64) and do.

### 3.2 H4b is mis-read in the abstract (interpretation; verdict unchanged)

H4b is "not supported" by its rule because output KL is not strictly increasing over the planted snapshots
(`results/controls/planted_calibration_T0.json`: KL 0 → 0.371 → 0.040 → 4.25e-4). The same file shows why: the
snapshot's own task accuracy is 1.000 → 0.977 → 0.996 → 1.000. Further training first perturbs and then restores the
planted model's *function*, and KL follows the function, as a divergence of outputs should; HS falls monotonically.
§5's gloss ("this would mean the grader is unreliable") assumed a monotone functional drift that did not happen, so
the test did not probe grader reliability as intended. §4.3 of the paper already said this; the abstract said the
opposite ("output KL does not behave as a calibrated distance"), and README and summary said KL "did not rise steadily
as it drifted". Fixed everywhere; the planted-mechanism figure now shows the snapshot accuracy (markers only, since
the snapshot axis is categorical).

### 3.3 H8 shows resemblance to the trained end point, not a separate tracking of circuit formation

The negative controls of deviation 9 (W-freq, E1, E0 replacements: ρ ≤ +0.14) showed that the correlation is specific to networks with T0's frequencies; they could not show what its size means. A positive control does. Final T0 itself graded against its own 401 checkpoints, T0 restricted to its key frequencies and the agent-free compiler's E3 build all rise with the restricted loss during circuit formation (Spearman ρ ≥ 0.997 over the 150 checkpoints up to step 14,900, a post-hoc split after which the restricted loss stays within 1.2 × its minimum), exactly as the six E3/E4 replacements do; an independent retrain falls (-1.00). After grokking (251 checkpoints) the restricted loss creeps up (5.2e-08 to 5.7e-08) while each network's HS drifts monotonically, and this plateau sets the size of the whole-trajectory ρ: final T0 itself gets 0.59, below every replacement (0.75–0.84), T0 restricted to its key frequencies 0.99, the compiler's E3 build 0.88 (`scripts/audit4/h8_positive_control.py`, `results/audit4/h8_positive_control.json`). H8's positive ρ therefore shows that the replacements share T0's frequency structure, not a separate ability of HS to track circuit formation, and its size does not order networks by faithfulness. The text in the abstract, README, summary, paper §4.3 and the round-2 report that said "HS tracks circuit formation" now says this; the trajectory figure draws final T0's own curve (black dashed). H8's preregistered verdict (every E3/E4 ρ > 0) stands. A test of tracking beyond resemblance would need, for example, a partial correlation given HS to the final model, or replacements of intermediate checkpoints.

### 3.4 Bootstrap intervals at three runs per arm are about 75 % intervals

With three runs, a percentile bootstrap of a mean resamples only 10 distinct multisets, and the all-minimum resample
alone has probability 1/27 > 2.5 %, so the nominal 95 % interval is exactly [min, max] of the three runs. Its true
coverage is 1 − 2·(1/2)³ = 75 % for a symmetric distribution; a simulation gives 0.744 for a mean and 0.815 for a
3-vs-3 difference (normal data, 3,000 trials; Hesterberg 2015, [arXiv:1411.5279](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.5279)).
The documents said these intervals are "too narrow"; they now state the coverage. This weakens H5's "beyond the
bootstrap CI" statements on the seed targets (four of eleven pairs) and the H2/H3 effect intervals; no verdict uses
them.

### 3.5 Leakage and contamination checks

- *Compiler:* `reference/compile.py` fits only to quantities in the explanation's parameter file (`spec.json`): the
  described neuron functions, the described logit form and the stated loss; no target weight or activation.
- *E0/E1:* byte-identical on T0, T1, T2, T-new, T-paper and dev, so their T0 and T-paper runs are replicates of one
  condition, as the memorisation analysis assumes.
- *Contamination:* the target files were uploaded on 2026-10-21 (Hugging Face API: repository created
  2026-10-21T01:08:45Z, first commit already contains `wd1_s1/analysis/key_freqs.json`); Claude Haiku 5.5's training
  data cutoff is June 2026 ([models overview](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/overview)) and it was released on
  2026-10-07 ([model page](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/haiku-5-5/overview)), before the files existed.
  The release date is a stronger argument than the cutoff and was added.
- *Ties:* scipy's exact Mann–Whitney ignores ties; none of the 36 values entering the reported rank tests are tied, so
  every reported p-value is exact (my enumeration with ½-ties gives the same values).

### 3.8 Smaller findings

- *Clock/Pizza wording.* `clock_pizza.py` called the compiler's E3 build the "literal Clock" although its distance
  irrelevance (0.18, `results/controls/clock_pizza.json`) is on the Pizza side; `progress.md` called the W-alg control
  "valid" without saying that E3 vs W-alg compares two Pizza-side builds (the paper said it). Zhong et al.'s Fig. 7
  boundaries are fitted separately per metric, not a joint rule; "Clock ≈ 1, Pizza ≈ 0" became their stated typical
  ranges.
- *Frozen W-alg text.* It says the "=" position receives 2cos(w_k(a−b)/2)·(unit vector) where attention weights 0.5
  give cos(w_k(a−b)/2)·(unit vector), and calls a single ReLU's positive part an "absolute value". Neither changes the
  algorithm described, and every W-alg build is Pizza-side and correct; the text is frozen protocol, so it is
  documented (`render.py` comment, deviation 11), not changed.
- *Negative-result wording.* "HS does not separate W-alg / W-scramble" now states the differences and intervals and
  that a failure to separate at N = 3 is not a demonstrated equivalence (no equivalence test was preregistered).
- *Exact zeros in H7.* All three E4 replacements score HS 0.000 against T1: at every site the held-out completeness R²
  is slightly negative (−0.002 to −0.026) and clipped to 0 (`results/audit4/regrade.json`); not a solver failure, and H7
  uses the margin to the best wrong target (T2).
- *Memorisation wording.* "The simulator built the published mechanism" now excepts the E1 run that chose its
  frequencies by random search (target-swap HS 0.24).
- *Code comments.* `stats.py` still said the JT test is exact up to 50,000 assignments (the code uses 400,000) and its
  Mann–Whitney docstring disagreed with the code; `metrics.py` said HS = 1 "exactly" (the ridge term keeps it just
  below) and CKA/Procrustes "only" orthogonal-invariant (also isotropic scaling); `sensitivity.py` cited the wrong
  Kornblith section for R²_CCA and omitted the dimension condition; the training docstring called a float64 loss part
  of the paper's recipe (the paper's notebook and ARENA use float64, the published `helpers.py` casts to float32).

## 6. Remaining limitations this audit could not remove

- N = 3 per condition; H2 cannot reach significance until round 3 (N = 10), which stays postponed.
- One task family, one simulator; the capability axis (H5 trend, H6) remains untestable.
- HS measures frequency-level structure; it does not see Clock vs Pizza or per-neuron assignments, and the rendered
  "true" explanations themselves compile to Pizza-side networks.
- The agents' repair and constant tuning against task labels cannot be removed from this task.
- The planted-mechanism control (H4b) and the trajectory correlation (H8) do not test the grader in the way §5
  intended; a planted series whose function drifts monotonically, and a trajectory test that conditions on similarity
  to the final model, would be needed.
